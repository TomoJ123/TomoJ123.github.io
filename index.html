Deep Current

A cozy idle fishing game for iPhone

Build a fishing fleet on a sunny Adriatic harbour, send your captains into ever deeper waters, and fill a book of 24 strange fish — even while you're away.

Support

A question, a bug, a lost purchase? Write to tomislavjurica123@gmail.com — please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. Purchases can always be restored in the game: Settings → Restore purchases.

Your privacy

No account, no name, no email needed to play. Read the privacy policy; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad choices any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
