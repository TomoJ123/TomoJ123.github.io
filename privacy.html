Deep Current — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Deep Current is a fishing-fleet idle game made by Tomislav Jurić-Arambašić in Croatia ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects and why. Short version: we don't collect your name, email, contacts or location. The game's optional reward videos come from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, and asks for your consent first where the law requires it.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your fleet, Charts, Pearls, species, settings) is saved on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your device, a copy of your save is kept in your own iCloud account so it can move with you to a new phone. We cannot read your iCloud data.

Notifications ("your ship is back", "your holds are full") are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us to deliver them.

Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the game, it sends anonymous usage statistics through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. These describe how the game is played — for example "first boat bought after 40 seconds", "sea 3", "a voyage to Kornati was sent", "a Pearl pack was bought" — together with general technical information (app version, iOS version, device model, language and region).

  • They are not linked to your identity: TelemetryDeck uses a random, salted and hashed identifier; we never see who you are.
  • They are not used for advertising or tracking.
  • You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous stats.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game only learns which item was bought, to deliver it, and counts purchases anonymously in the statistics above.

Advertising

Deep Current offers optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one in return for something in the game (a catch boost, a doubled welcome-back haul, time off a voyage, a Pearl). Ads are never needed to play, and the "No Ads" purchase makes those rewards free without any video.

The videos are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the game may collect your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), information about your device and how ads were shown and tapped, crash and performance data, and — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" keeps the IDFA from the game and from Google.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland (or a US state with its own privacy law), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, the first time ads become available — and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Deep Current is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to look up, export or delete. You can delete everything the game stores by deleting the app (and its data in iCloud via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage). If you contact us, we use your email only to answer. For the data Google processes to show ads, see Google's policy linked above; you can also reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You have the right to complain to a data protection authority — in Croatia, AZOP.
